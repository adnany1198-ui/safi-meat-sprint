## 4. OUR PROCESS PAGE (process.html)

### Page Header
- **Label:** Our Process
- **Heading:** Farm to Shipment

### Hero Visual — Supply Chain Map
- **Label:** Interactive Supply Chain Map
- An interactive geographic map showing farm locations across Punjab, processing facilities, cold chain routes, and directional flow from source to export ports and destination markets. Hover over any node to see details about that stage of the supply chain.

### 7-Step Process Flow

**Step 01 — Controlled Sourcing**
- Sourced from our own farms and vetted partner farms across Punjab's livestock belt. We control breed selection, feed quality, and animal health from the start.
- Overseen by: Livestock & Sourcing Team

**Step 02 — Approved Processing Partners**
- All processing occurs at export-certified, halal-compliant facilities that meet strict international hygiene standards.
- Overseen by: Processing Operations

**Step 03 — Quality Inspection**
- Multi-stage quality checks at pre-slaughter, during processing, and post-packaging. Every batch is graded before it advances.
- Overseen by: Quality Assurance

**Step 04 — Cold Chain Integrity**
- Temperature-controlled storage and transport from processing facility to port. No breaks in the chain.
- Overseen by: Logistics Team

**Step 05 — Packaging**
- Vacuum-sealed, export-grade packaging with full labelling — product type, cut, weight, batch number, processing date, and halal certification.
- Overseen by: Packaging & QA

**Step 06 — Documentation**
- Complete export documentation including health certificates, halal certificates, phytosanitary documents, commercial invoices, and customs clearance.
- Overseen by: Export Documentation

**Step 07 — Shipment (Air & Sea)**
- Final dispatch via air freight for urgent orders or sea freight for bulk shipments. Full tracking provided to buyer from departure to arrival.
- Overseen by: Logistics & Freight
