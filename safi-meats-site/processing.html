Selection Standards

Facility Selection Criteria

Every processing facility is selected and retained against the following fixed standards. Non-compliance on any criterion results in suspension pending corrective action.

Halal Certification Current halal certification from an approved certifying body, with named certificate and expiry date on file
Hygiene & HACCP HACCP-compliant processing environment, subject to regular third-party audit
Export Licence Licensed for international meat export by the relevant national authority
Cold Chain Continuous temperature monitoring from processing through to dispatch; chilled product held at 0–4°C, frozen at −18°C or below
Animal Welfare Compliance with halal slaughter requirements and humane handling protocols at all stages
Traceability Full batch-level traceability from livestock origin through processing to packaged product
Capacity & Reliability Demonstrated ability to meet order volumes and dispatch timelines consistently

Certification documentation is available on request for compliance, import and audit requirements. Contact info@safimeats.com or WhatsApp 03003996998.
