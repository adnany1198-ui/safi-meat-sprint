Who We Are

Built for Global Scale

Safi Meats is a premium meat exporter and direct international supplier delivering high-quality protein to global markets across the Middle East and Asia.

We operate with full control across sourcing, quality assurance, documentation, and shipment execution — ensuring reliability at every stage of the supply chain.

Led by a commercially experienced leadership team with deep roots in agriculture, trade, and international markets, the company is built for long-term global growth.
