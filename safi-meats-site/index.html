Who We Are

Pakistan has one of the largest livestock populations in the world.

Yet the global market continues to face a shortage of consistently high-quality, fully traceable meat. Much of the industry operates without structured farming, breeding, and quality systems required to meet international standards at scale.

Safi Meats was established to change that.

We are building a fully integrated supply model centred around our own farms and controlled sourcing to ensure traceability, consistency, and product integrity from origin to shipment.

Our long-term vision is to elevate the global perception of Pakistani meat and position it as a reliable, premium export product capable of competing internationally.

Mission
To supply global markets exclusively with meat sourced from our own farms, delivered with disciplined execution and absolute reliability — because consistency is our currency of credibility.
Our Capability

Built for Global Scale
